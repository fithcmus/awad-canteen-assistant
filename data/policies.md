# Canteen B — rules for students and staff (fictional, for the course demos)

## P1 Opening days
Canteen B is open Monday to Saturday. Each stall keeps its own hours; the order screen shows them. On public holidays the whole canteen is closed, including the Lunar New Year week, Reunification Day (April 30) and National Day (September 2).

## P2 Pre-orders
A pre-order is placed at least 30 minutes before pick-up and at most 2 days ahead. A pre-order is for one pick-up time at one counter; items from several stalls are collected together at counter 2.

## P3 Paying
Orders are paid from the campus wallet when the order is placed. Each member pays for their own items. Cash is accepted only at the counter, never for pre-orders.

## P4 Late or wrong orders
If a pre-order is not ready within 15 minutes of the pick-up time, or an item is wrong, show the order code at counter 2. The price of that item goes back to the wallet the same day. Complaints after the day of pick-up are not accepted.

## P5 Cancelling
A pre-order can be cancelled in the app until 60 minutes before pick-up, and the full amount goes back to each member's wallet. After that, cancelling is not possible.

## P6 Allergies
Allergen labels are set by each stall. Kitchens share equipment, so a label is not a guarantee. A student with a severe allergy should talk to the stall before ordering.

## P7 Vegetarian food
A dish marked vegetarian has no meat, fish or fish sauce. Green Bowl serves only vegetarian dishes. Other stalls may cook vegetarian dishes in shared pans.

## P8 Group orders
One member places a group order for up to 6 people. Every member must accept before the wallets are charged; until then the order is a draft that only its creator can see.

## P9 Wallet top-up
The wallet is topped up at counter 1 or by bank transfer in the student app. The smallest top-up is 20,000 VND. A balance is never paid back in cash.

## P10 Lost property
Things left in the canteen are kept at counter 1 for 7 days, then handed to the faculty office.
